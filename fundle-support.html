Fundle Plus & Subscription Support

Managing or Canceling Subscriptions:

  • Apple (iOS): Open iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Fundle Plus.
  • Google Play (Android): Open Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Fundle Plus.

Restoring Purchases: If you reinstalled Fundle or switched devices, open the Fundle Plus upgrade sheet in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

Account & Data Deletion Requests

You have full ownership of your data. You can delete your account and all associated records directly inside the app under My Fundle → App Settings → Delete Account.

Alternatively, you may request manual deletion by emailing support.fundle@gmail.com from your registered account email with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
