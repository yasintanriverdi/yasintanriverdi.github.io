Fundle Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 20, 2026

Fundle helps users manage their personal finances, wallets, budgets, and transactions across multiple devices.

Information We Collect

Fundle requires an account to use and stores your financial data (such as wallets, transactions, budgets, and custom categories) securely on our cloud servers. We also collect your email address for authentication purposes.

The app uses third-party services such as Firebase Auth, Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google AdMob, which may collect limited technical and diagnostic information, analytics data, coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, and app interaction data to support app functionality, stability, and ad delivery.

How We Use Information

We use your information strictly to operate the app, sync your data across your devices, improve performance, understand app usage, and display ads where applicable.

Local Data

Your financial data is securely stored and associated with your account. We do not sell your personal financial data to third parties. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.
